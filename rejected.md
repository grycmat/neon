About the Child Safety Standards policy
To keep Google Play a safe platform, we maintain a Child Safety Standards policy that requires all social and dating apps to have and maintain published standards that explicitly prohibit child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), provide the contact information of a child safety-specific point of contact, and properly declare that these apps have an in-app mechanism for user feedback, a method for addressing CSAM, and comply with all applicable child safety laws and regulations.
