Neon is a fast, modern, and beautiful Mastodon client built natively for Android with Jetpack Compose.

Features:
• Clean and fluid UI with dark, light, and wallpaper-based dynamic theming
• Adaptive multi-pane layouts for foldables, tablets, and large screens
• Fast timeline navigation with home, local, federated, explore, and direct message feeds
• Push notifications with on-device RFC 8291 end-to-end payload decryption
• Full de-googled / FOSS support with open UnifiedPush push providers (e.g. ntfy)
• Rich media viewer, polls, bookmarks, and account management
• Zero third-party trackers or telemetry
