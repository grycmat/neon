# Google Ads Campaign — Neon (Android Mastodon Client)

App: **Neon** — native Android Mastodon client (`com.gigapingu.neon`)
Recommended format: **Google App Campaign** (Performance Max for App / "app install" goal), since the objective is Play Store installs.

## Google Ads requirements this content follows (2026)

- **Headlines**: up to 5, **max 30 characters each**. Mandatory.
- **Descriptions**: up to 5, **max 90 characters each**. Mandatory.
- Every headline/description must **make sense standalone** — Google auto-combines them, so don't write text that depends on another line for context. One value proposition per line.
- Images: 3 aspect ratios — **1200×628** (1.91:1 landscape), **1200×1200** (1:1 square), **480×800** (portrait, optional). <5MB each, up to 20 images/ad group. Recommended: 2 of each.
- Video: 16:9 landscape, 1:1 square, 9:16/2:3 portrait. **10–30s** recommended, hosted on YouTube (linked, not uploaded directly). 1080p preferred, min 480p.
- Don't pull creative in the first 2 weeks — let assets exit "Learning" before judging performance.

Sources: [Google Ads Help — App campaign assets](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6357595), [Google Ads Help — creative best practices](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6167158), [megadigital.ai spec guide](https://megadigital.ai/en/blog/google-app-campaign-specs/)

## Positioning

Neon's real differentiators vs. generic Mastodon apps and vs. X/Twitter:
1. **No ads, no tracking, no third-party analytics** — genuinely, not just marketing copy (see `policy.html`).
2. **Chronological, algorithm-free feed** — timelines aren't ranked/manipulated.
3. **Native Android** (Kotlin/Compose) — fast, not a wrapped web view.
4. **Distinctive "glassy neon" design** + optional Material You wallpaper theming.
5. **Full feature set**: DMs, lists, filters, polls, bookmarks, push notifications, tablet/foldable adaptive layouts.

Target audience: people leaving X/Twitter for privacy or algorithm-fatigue reasons, existing Mastodon/fediverse users wanting a polished native app, Android power users (foldables/tablets).

## Visual asset suggestions

- **Screenshots to capture**: Home timeline (glassy card + gradient background), Notifications feed, Profile with the neon gradient header, Composer with CW/poll toggle, Settings showing "Match wallpaper colors" (Material You) on/off, a tablet/foldable list-detail view.
- **App icon**: 1024×1024 (already have Neon's launcher icon).
- **Square (1200×1200) / Landscape (1200×628)**: pair a phone screenshot with a short headline overlay, e.g. "No Ads. No Tracking." or "Bez reklam i śledzenia."
- **Portrait (480×800)**: single full-bleed phone screenshot of the timeline, no text overlay needed.
- **Video (10–30s, YouTube)**: quick scroll through the glassy timeline → tap into a thread → show notification arriving → end on the app icon + "Neon" wordmark. One EN cut, one PL-subtitled or PL-voiceover cut.
